Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Crovelle Living Forms ("we", "our", "us") uses minimal cookies to ensure the proper functioning of our website and to improve your browsing experience. This policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them.

Our commitment: We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party marketing cookies. Our analytics are privacy-first and anonymised. Your reading experience is not for sale.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep your session secure, and understand how visitors use the site. Cookies do not contain personal information unless you have provided it (for example, by subscribing to our newsletter).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use only two categories of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the website to function. They enable core features such as page navigation, security, and session management. Without these, the site cannot operate properly.
  • Analytics cookies: We use a self-hosted, privacy-first analytics tool to understand which articles are most read and how visitors navigate the site. This data is aggregated and anonymous — it never identifies you personally and is not shared with third parties.

3. Specific Cookies We Use

The table below lists the specific cookies you may encounter on our site:

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
session_id Essential Maintains your browsing session and ensures secure page transitions. Session
csrf_token Essential Protects against cross-site request forgery and form tampering. Session
preferences Essential Stores lightweight preferences such as your cookie consent choice. 1 year
_pk_id (analytics) Analytics Anonymised unique identifier used to distinguish visitors for statistics. Not linked to personal data. 13 months
_pk_ses (analytics) Analytics Short-lived session cookie for analytics, counting visits and page views anonymously. 30 minutes

We do not use any cookies from Google Analytics, Facebook, or other third-party advertising networks. Our analytics tool (based on Matomo, self-hosted) is configured to anonymise IP addresses and respect "Do Not Track" browser signals.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The steps vary by browser; check your browser's "Help" or "Settings" section. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of the site.
  • Do Not Track: Our analytics automatically respects the "Do Not Track" (DNT) header sent by your browser. If you have DNT enabled, we will not track your visit.
  • Opt-out links: Since we use a self-hosted analytics tool, there is no third-party opt-out link required. You may simply enable DNT or block analytics cookies in your browser.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We do not allow third-party advertising or tracking cookies on our site. However, we may embed content from external platforms (for example, Vimeo or YouTube) in some articles. These platforms may set their own cookies when you interact with the embedded content. We do not control those cookies; please refer to the respective platform's cookie policy for details.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
