    • About Crovelle

    Independent editorial on interior design, architecture, and the craft of space — written for professionals and thoughtful homeowners alike.

    Crovelle Living Forms is an independent editorial publication dedicated to the study and appreciation of interior design and architecture. Founded in 2018, we publish in-depth articles, practical guides, and critical essays that explore how we shape the spaces we inhabit — and how they shape us.

    Our editorial team is based across Europe and North America, drawing on backgrounds in architecture, interior design, journalism, and material science. We believe that good design writing should be accessible, rigorous, and beautiful. Too often, design media falls into one of two traps: either it is superficial, reducing complex spaces to pretty pictures, or it is impenetrable, written only for a narrow professional elite.

    Crovelle exists to bridge that gap. We explain principles, evaluate materials, and analyse spaces with clarity and depth. Whether you are an architect specifying finishes for a residential project, a designer looking for practical guidance, or a homeowner planning a renovation, we aim to give you knowledge you can actually use.

    Our Mission

    We exist to bridge the gap between professional practice and public understanding. Too much design media is either superficial or inaccessible. Crovelle aims to be different: we explain principles, evaluate materials, and analyse spaces with clarity and depth.

    Every article we publish begins with a simple question: what does a thoughtful person need to know about this topic? That might mean unpacking the difference between engineered and solid wood flooring, exploring the psychology of lighting colour temperature, or examining why courtyard houses are making a comeback in dense cities.

    “Good design is not about decoration. It is about how a space supports the life that happens within it.”

    Editorial Independence

    We do not accept sponsored content, and we do not allow advertisers to influence our editorial decisions. Our recommendations are based on research, experience, and a genuine commitment to good design. When we evaluate a material or a method, we do so on its merits — not on the basis of who is paying for advertising.

    This independence is funded entirely by our readers through newsletter subscriptions and voluntary contributions. It allows us to remain accountable to you, not to commercial interests.

    Our Commitment to You

    We are committed to publishing articles that are accurate, useful, and a pleasure to read. We correct our mistakes promptly and transparently. We do not use AI to generate articles — every piece is written, edited, and fact-checked by a human being.
